Demonstrations and events

2012

Equifest 2012 — 22 September, Orangeville Fairgrounds. Table and information on craniosacral therapy for horses and riders, organized by the Women’s Horse Owner Association (W.H.O.A.).

November — Royal Agricultural Winter Fair, Toronto. Three demos on two days in the Educational Ring of the Ontario Equestrian Federation “Spirit of the Horse” exhibit.

2011

29 May — Demo at the Trillium Hunter Jumper Show at Greystone, Collingwood/Stayner.

5 June — Equine craniosacral therapy at Equifest, Orangeville Fairgrounds, organized by W.H.O.A.

2010

16 February — W.H.O.A. meeting at Petlyn Farm: “Benefits of Bodywork and demonstrations,” presented with Liz Sleight, Bowen and EMRT practitioner.

6 June — Equine massage demo at Equifest, Orangeville Fairgrounds.

November — Educational demonstrations at the Royal Agricultural Winter Fair with the Ontario Equestrian Federation (November 6, 8, and 12).

2009

3 October — Equine massage demo at Loralee Farms as part of the Hills of the Headwaters Stable Tour.

17 October — Equine massage demo at Troy Vet Clinic.

23 October — Liw on Rogers TV Daytime (Barrie), talking about equine-assisted learning and personal development.

21 November — Equine massage demo at Harmony Hills Stable, west of Collingwood.

13 December — Equine massage demo at Pine Hollow Stables, south of Cambridge.

New events go up on the home page when they’re confirmed. If you’d like me to come to your barn for a demonstration, just ask.
