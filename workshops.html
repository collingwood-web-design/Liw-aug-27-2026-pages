Services

Equine-facilitated personal development

There is so much we can learn about ourselves beside a horse. As prey animals, they notice everything — the weather, the other animals in the field, and us.

When we work with horses as facilitators, we get an honest picture of how we come across: how we move, how we listen, how we ask. That kind of day helps people show up a little more themselves — at home, and at work.

If you have a team, a workplace group, or a circle of friends who want a day with horses, write me. It’s the kind of day people still talk about.

Possible workshop foci

Workshops are scheduled as customized events. Topics can include:

  • Understanding trust and respect
  • Defining and maintaining boundaries
  • Overcoming fear
  • Communication skills
  • Listening
  • Leadership

Email findingquiet@gmail.com and tell me what you have in mind. I’ll send a quote.
