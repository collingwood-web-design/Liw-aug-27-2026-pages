Services

Equine-facilitated personal development

There is so much we can learn about ourselves by working with our equine partners. As prey animals, horses are very sensitive to the environment and the other animals in it — including humans.

Working with equine facilitators, we can learn how we are perceived and how we move and communicate with others. This kind of personal and professional development supports you in becoming more genuine and confident, in both personal and professional settings.

Contact Liw to discuss an event for your team or workplace. This is corporate training you will not forget.

Possible workshop foci

Workshops are scheduled as customized events. Topics can include:

  • Understanding trust and respect
  • Defining and maintaining boundaries
  • Overcoming fear
  • Communication skills
  • Listening
  • Leadership
