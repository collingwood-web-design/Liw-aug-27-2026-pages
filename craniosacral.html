Craniosacral therapy for you

CST · Upledger Institute · Collingwood

Liw (short for Liwana) Bringelson, PhD CST, has been an advocate — and client — of Craniosacral Therapy for many years, and a practitioner since 2008. Her goal is to support you and your body to find the internal quiet that lets you realize the full benefits of your body’s self-healing properties.

It is too easy to get caught up in the hustle of everyday life and function at less than optimal levels — physically, mentally, and emotionally. Many people find that Craniosacral Therapy helps them slow down, rebalance, and reconnect to the inner wisdom that can deal better with stress, reduce pain, and support wellness.

Her background includes human factors, ergonomics, social psychology, and engineering. She earned a PhD from Purdue University in Industrial Engineering and cognitive human factors, and later worked as university faculty in the US and Canada. Finding Quiet continues those objectives: an engineering problem-solving mind paired with an intuitive, listening approach to your body.

In December 2012, Liw completed her CST-techniques certification through the Upledger Institute. She has served as a Teaching Assistant for multiple Upledger courses (including CS I, CS II, SER I, and EcoSomatics Equine I) and is recognized as an Upledger Approved Craniosacral Therapy Study Group Leader. Ongoing study has included Therapeutic Touch, SomatoEmotional Release, and Advanced Craniosacral Therapy.

If you are experiencing chronic tension, nervous system dysregulation, post-concussion symptoms, migraines, or persistent stress, Craniosacral Therapy may be a supportive addition to your care plan.

Craniosacral therapy for you is carried out at Collaborative Health Group in Collingwood. Book online for office appointments. On-site and show sessions can also be arranged.

Equine bodywork, workshops, and all other treatments are arranged by contacting Liw directly: findingquiet@gmail.com or (705) 888-5290.
