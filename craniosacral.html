Human craniosacral treatments

Craniosacral work is quiet, hands-on help — an invitation for your body to remember how to settle and heal. I trained with the Upledger Institute, and I work with you toward balance, and toward letting go of old patterns and chronic pain.

Coming back regularly helps your body keep that ease — and that matters for riders. Horses notice the small shifts in our mood, energy, and balance.

If you’d like a longer conversation about human CST, I keep a dedicated craniosacral site.
