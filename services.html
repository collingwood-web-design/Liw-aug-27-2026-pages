Services

Help horses and people realize their potential.

Finding Quiet Equine provides services to help horses and people realize their potential. Schedule appointments for both your horse and yourself to address the movement patterns that limit your partnership.

Human craniosacral

Horses pick up small shifts in our mood, energy, and balance. This subtle hands-on modality can help your body regain and maintain balance as you work and play.
