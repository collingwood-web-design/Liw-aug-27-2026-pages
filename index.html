The work

Inner quiet, for horse and rider.

Finding Quiet supports you and your horse in finding the quiet that lets you connect and work together efficiently.

Liw does this through equine bodywork, helping your horse find his inner quiet, and by designing and co-facilitating workshops with horses so you can explore your own inner quiet — even if you don’t own a horse. She applies the principles of craniosacral therapy (CST) in her work with animals and humans.

This site discusses the process and results of equine craniosacral therapy, and the way Liw works with your horse. For more on human CST, visit her dedicated craniosacral website.

Every discipline

From trail horses to Grand Prix.

There are many types of equine bodywork, and they can benefit all types and levels of horses. A Grand Prix dressage horse may receive regular treatments, as well as a beloved trail horse. Just as human bodies absorb — and defend against — the effects of work and daily life, so do our horses’ bodies.

A regularly scheduled massage helps your horse deal with the stress and strain of the job. A series of treatments may help overcome a long-term injury or movement pattern. Your horse can benefit regardless of discipline, English or Western.

Liw has worked with western trail horses and barrel racers, horses trained in dressage and hunter/jumper eventing, and even a polo horse or two.
